Kanji Claro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026
Developer: Daruen Software
Privacy contact: daruensoftware@gmail.com

Data stored by the app

Kanji Claro does not require an account and does not operate its own server to store study progress. Lesson level, kanji marked as known, the review list, and cached translations are stored in the app's private preferences on the device. Android may include this data in device backups depending on device and backup-account settings. Local progress is removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; system backups are governed by Android and the backup provider's settings and policies.

Translations with Google ML Kit

When translations are needed, Kanji Claro downloads the relevant language model from Google and translates locally stored kanji glosses on the device. Google states that translation input and output are processed on-device and are not sent to its servers.

The ML Kit SDK may send Google usage and diagnostic metrics, such as device and app information, installation identifiers, performance, SDK events and errors, input/output sizes, and configured source and target languages. Google processes these data to maintain, measure, and improve ML Kit and detect abuse under its own terms and privacy policy. Language models and updates are downloaded from Google. See ML Kit terms and privacy and ML Kit Android data disclosures.

To manage model availability and downloads, Google's libraries may communicate with Firebase services. Those requests may include app installation identifiers and technical information such as app version, Android version, language, country, and time zone. Google describes these practices in its Firebase Android data disclosure.

Writing, voice, and external links

Handwriting strokes are processed locally and are not sent to a Kanji Claro server. Pronunciation uses the device's text-to-speech service. If you choose to support the project, the app opens Ko-fi in your browser. Ko-fi and your selected payment provider apply their own privacy policies and terms. Kanji Claro does not receive your card details.

Retention and contact

Study progress and cached translations remain in the app's storage until you clear app data or uninstall it. Kanji Claro does not have an account that links this progress to an identity. For privacy questions, contact daruensoftware@gmail.com. Diagnostic data collected by Google through ML Kit is also governed by Google's policies.

Other providers

  • Google ML Kit and Google Play Services: translations, model downloads, and technical/diagnostic metrics.
  • Android and the backup provider configured on the device: possible backup of the app's private storage.
  • Ko-fi and PayPal, Stripe, or other payment providers: only if you open the support link and choose to contribute.

The kanji and stroke datasets included in the app are not user personal data. Their sources and licenses are listed in the third-party notices.
